## What changed against COMMODORE

COMMODORE's window was **five generated images** — `cm_wx_clear`, `_night`,
`_overcast`, `_rain`, `_snow` — each a whole scene with the sky, the ground,
the aircraft symbol and the pitch ladder baked together.

**None of them are used here.** The window is now nine procedural layers and
sixteen states, and the states cost hex colours rather than generations. On
the original plan that was 17 states × 4 faces = **68 new AI images**; it is
now **zero**.

So the most characterful part of the face — the view out of the window — has
moved from the "cannot ship" column to the "can" column. What still blocks
this face is the plate and the hands, which are unchanged from COMMODORE and
still generated. See `../commodore/PROVENANCE.md`.

`pw_surround` is honest about being derived: it is COMMODORE's own plate with
a hole cut in it, and the hole is taken from the alpha of COMMODORE's own
window layer so the aperture cannot drift from the art. It inherits the
plate's provenance. When the plate is rebuilt, the surround is regenerated
from the rebuilt one and becomes clean with it.

## The replacement route

Unchanged from the rest of the line: the Blender instrument kit in the
studio's `roadmap/DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md`. **The window no longer needs it** —
only the plate and the hands do. That is a materially smaller job than it was
before this face existed, and it is the first time the PRO argument has been
demonstrated rather than asserted.
