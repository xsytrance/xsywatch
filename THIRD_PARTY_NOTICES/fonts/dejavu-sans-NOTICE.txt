NOTICE — DejaVu Sans Bold (provenance id: dejavu-sans-bold)
===========================================================

Raster glyphs in this repository are rendered from DejaVu Sans Bold. They are
rasterised into PNGs at build time and shipped as WFF `BitmapFont` characters;
the TTF itself is NOT redistributed. (Custom TTFs do not render in Watch Face
Format on the Galaxy Watch 7, so a pre-rendered bitmap font is the only route
that works.)

Where it is used:

  tools/make_probe_assets.py       MERIDIAN PROBE — full A-Z, 0-9, punctuation
  tools/make_subdial_furniture.py  sub-dial furniture across the MERIDIAN line
  tools/render_face_from_xml.py    review-image labels only (not shipped)
